Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At LifeBridge, your paperwork privacy is non-negotiable. Official documents often contain sensitive personal information, and our architecture is designed to respect and protect your data.

1. Zero-Retention Client Demos

Documents uploaded to this interactive web demonstration are processed entirely in-memory within your local browser session. No files or text are transmitted to permanent remote databases.

2. Ephemeral Production Analysis

When using our authenticated mobile or backend services, files are analyzed in temporary encrypted compute environments for text parsing and entity extraction. Once your action checklist is returned to your device, source files are purged.

3. No Data Selling or Public Training

We do not sell personal data, nor do we use your private government notices or certificates to train public AI foundation models.

Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Informational & Reading Assistance Only

LifeBridge is an artificial intelligence reading assistant created to help parse and summarize complex paperwork. It does not provide legal advice, financial counseling, or official government certifications.

2. Verification of Deadlines and Criteria

While LifeBridge strives for maximum extraction precision, users must always verify critical application deadlines, eligibility criteria, and fee structures against the original government circular or official department portal before submitting applications.
